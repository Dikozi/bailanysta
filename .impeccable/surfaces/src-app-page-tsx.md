# Surface: landing page `/`

Scope: single-page promo site for My Assist. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: busy specialists and entrepreneurs, mostly on phones, arriving from Instagram/Telegram.
Action: message the owner in Telegram (https://t.me/diaskadyrbekov).
Proof on hand: none real. All chat/calendar demos are reconstructed from the bot's real message templates and labeled illustrative.
Constraints: Russian only, «ты»; no invented metrics, testimonials, comparisons; pricing 35 000 ₸ / prepay 15 000 ₸ / 7 days support.
Memorable moment: the hero chat demo where one sentence turns into tasks, a calendar event and a reminder.
Unresolved: final logo, confirmed Instagram link, account ownership model, legal entity.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category-standard dark AI-product landing, executed at Linear / Raycast / Superhuman finish. The page owns one idea: a sentence you send in Telegram becomes tasks, a Google Calendar event and a reminder — shown live, not claimed. It refuses the gradient-blob hero with a static phone screenshot and a grid of generic icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool ink ground (deep navy-black derived from #001536), layered surfaces separated by hairlines at low alpha, a single sky-blue accent for action and bot identity, mint reserved strictly for "done" states, amber only for reminders. Product UI is rendered in real HTML at full fidelity: Telegram-like chat bubbles, inline buttons, a day-column calendar, notification toasts. Onest variable for all text (tight display tracking), JetBrains Mono for times and commands.

STORY: In seconds the visitor sees what it is (a personal planner bot in Telegram), watches it work on a real-sounding day, learns that a person sets it up for them, sees the exact price, and taps "Написать в Telegram".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop two columns. Left: small kicker, two-line headline «Меньше держать в голове. Больше успевать.», one-sentence subline, primary button "Написать в Telegram" plus secondary "Как это работает", price line. Right, larger: the live demo window — chat with a voice/text message resolving into saved-task cards, a calendar column where the 10:00 event lands, a reminder toast. Example-phrase chips below replay it. Mobile: text and button first, demo directly under.

FORM: Standing exit — category canon at full fidelity (owner declined rolled direction 7 "thermal receipt" and pick "tear-off calendar"); seed key 53ab5f65, degraded roll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
